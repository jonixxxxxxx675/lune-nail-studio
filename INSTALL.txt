LUNE mobile update

1. Upload mobile-enhancements.css and mobile-enhancements.js to the repository root.
2. In index.html, immediately after:
   <script src="intro.js" defer></script>
   add:
   <script src="mobile-enhancements.js" defer></script>

Only mobile behavior is affected: the new stylesheet is wrapped in @media (max-width:767px), and the JS exits on desktop.

The update adds:
- rounded top corners on the mobile hero;
- smoother intro scene transitions and one global loading bar;
- no blue tap highlight;
- premium 3D press states;
- working account registration gate before booking;
- My Account / My Bookings / My QR tabs;
- local demo booking history until Supabase is connected;
- QR generation via QRCode.js CDN;
- upgraded mobile footer shortcuts;
- menu shortcuts for account, bookings and QR.
